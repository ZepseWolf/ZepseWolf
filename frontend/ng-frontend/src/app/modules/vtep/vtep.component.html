Introduction

Using Angular 12 and the Electron framework, I successfully developed a fully interactive desktop application for managing Wire-Bond Capacitance Testing technology. I participated in an Agile development process utilizing Slack, JIRA, and Bitbucket to ensure a smooth and coordinated workflow.

I was primarily responsible for developing the frontend and the Electron application, including MQTT topic subscription handling. To test and validate the implementation, I also worked with Node-RED and RabbitMQ.
