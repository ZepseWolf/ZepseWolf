Introduction

Using Flask, MongoDB, and SQL, we successfully developed a fully interactive application designed to give competitive Digimon players an edge against their opponents.

I was primarily responsible for the login and registration pages, as well as the logic for comparing two Digimon to determine the likely winner. The project required us to work across both the frontend and backend to deliver the desired result.
