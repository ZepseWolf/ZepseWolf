Singapore's GDP growth appears to be slowing, as evidenced by decreasing inflation and lowered interest rates aimed at encouraging spending. Comparing two global currencies against the SGD, the Japanese Yen's value is declining rapidly while the Korean Won's value is gradually strengthening.

This suggests the Japanese Yen may be on a downward trajectory, as it continues to fall even against a slowing economy like Singapore's. In summary, at the time of this study, it would be advisable to avoid investing in the Japanese Yen.
